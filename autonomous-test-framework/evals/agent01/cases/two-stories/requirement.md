# Shopping Cart — Requirements

## Story 1

**Story ID:** CART-1
**As a** shopper **I want to** add a product to my cart **so that** I can buy it later.

Acceptance criteria:
1. Each product card has an **Add to cart** button.
2. Clicking **Add to cart** increases the cart badge count by 1.
3. Adding the same product twice shows quantity 2 on the cart page.

## Story 2

**Story ID:** CART-2
**As a** shopper **I want to** remove a product from my cart **so that** I only buy what I need.

Acceptance criteria:
1. Each cart line has a **Remove** button.
2. Clicking **Remove** deletes the line and decreases the cart badge count.
3. Removing the last product shows the message `Your cart is empty`.
